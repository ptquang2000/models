# LR-0003: The combination set is a shape, not a formula

Status: current

On 2026-11-25 the answer `{cu + dv, c, d ∈ ℝ}` was given as the set of all combinations.
That is the definition, not the set. The same gap appeared on 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24, so
notation was not the blocker.

A numeric drill closed it. Two pairs of vectors, three points each, then a reachability
question. The learner now states the plane case and the line case in words. He found the
`y = 0` obstruction in the parallel pair without help. The book sentence that states this is
Strang p. 1 and §1.1 p. 2, points 4–5. He asked for that citation before he accepted the
question.

Two defects remain. He writes "transformed" for "scalar multiple". He also argues the plane
case from the word "plane", not from the count of reachable points.

Teaching consequence: the span picture is now usable, so §2.1 and §3.1 can build on it
without a repeat. Check the exact word "scalar multiple" when §3.4 arrives.
